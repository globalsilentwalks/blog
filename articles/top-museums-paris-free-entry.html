12 Paris Museums You Can Visit for Free in 2026

📅 Updated May 2026 👤 GlobalSilentWalks Team ⏱ 8 min read

Paris has more free museums than any major capital. EU residents under 26 enter many state museums for free year-round, and the Petit Palais, Maison de Victor Hugo and Musée Carnavalet are free for everyone, every day.

336×280 In-Article Ad — AdSense Ready

Always free, for everyone

  • Petit Palais — fine arts, central, beautiful courtyard café.
  • Musée Carnavalet — history of Paris, Marais.
  • Maison de Victor Hugo — Place des Vosges.
  • Musée Cognacq-Jay — 18th-century art.
  • Musée Bourdelle — sculptor's atelier near Montparnasse.
  • Musée Zadkine — sculpture garden.
  • Maison de Balzac — Passy.

Free first Sunday of the month

The Louvre, Musée d'Orsay, Centre Pompidou, Musée Rodin, Musée Picasso. Reservation required online; book 1 month ahead.

Free for under-26s (EU residents)

All national museums, including the Louvre, Orsay, Pompidou, Versailles palace (gardens always free), Sainte-Chapelle, Conciergerie, Pantheon. Bring ID.

Free at all times for under-18s

Same list as above, regardless of nationality.

Hidden free spots

  • Atelier Brancusi next to Pompidou — free.
  • Musée de la Vie Romantique — Rue Chaptal.
  • Pavillon de l'Arsenal — Paris architecture.

Best strategy

Hit always-free museums on weekdays (less crowded) and book the free Louvre Sunday slot the moment it opens (1st of previous month).
