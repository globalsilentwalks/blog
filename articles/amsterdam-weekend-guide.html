Amsterdam Weekend Guide 2026: 48 Hours Done Right

📅 Updated May 2026 👤 GlobalSilentWalks Team ⏱ 8 min read

Three days is the sweet spot for a first visit to Paris in 2026. With Notre-Dame fully reopened, the Métro running 24/7 on weekends and a pedestrianized Seine, the city has never been easier to explore on foot. Here is the itinerary we recommend to friends.

A 48-hour Amsterdam itinerary covering canals, Van Gogh, Jordaan cafés and the best day trip to Zaanse Schans.

Friday evening

Drop bags, walk the Jordaan district, dinner at Café de Klepel.

Saturday

Van Gogh Museum at 9 a.m. (book ahead), canal cruise at noon, Albert Cuyp market in the afternoon, drinks on a houseboat at sunset.

Sunday

Day trip to Zaanse Schans windmills (20 min by train), back for stroopwafels at Lanskroon.

Where to stay

  • Budget: ClinkNOORD hostel
  • Mid: Hotel V Nesplein
  • Splurge: Pulitzer Amsterdam

Getting around

Rent a bike (€12/day). The tram works too. Avoid driving entirely. Compare with our Paris itinerary.
