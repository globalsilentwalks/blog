Bordeaux Wine Tour on a Budget: Vineyards Under €40 (2026)

📅 Updated May 2026 👤 GlobalSilentWalks Team ⏱ 8 min read

Bordeaux makes some of the world's most expensive wines, but visiting the vineyards does not have to be. With a bit of planning, you can taste at five châteaux for less than €40 total. Here is how.

336×280 In-Article Ad — AdSense Ready

Use the Bordeaux City Pass

The 2-day pass (€55) includes the Cité du Vin museum, public transport, plus discounted vineyard tours.

Take the local train, not a tour bus

Bordeaux to Saint-Émilion: 35 min, €10 round trip. Bordeaux to Pauillac (Médoc): 1h, €13. Group tours charge €120+ for the same route.

Free and cheap tastings

  • Maison du Vin de Saint-Émilion — €25 for 2 hours including tastings of 4 grands crus.
  • Château Soutard — €15 tour and tasting.
  • Bordeaux Wine Office downtown — free 30-min tasting class daily.

Pick small family châteaux

Skip the famous classified growths (€80+ tastings). Family estates in Entre-deux-Mers or Côtes de Bourg charge €5-10 and pour you twice as much.

Best regions for budget travelers

  • Saint-Émilion — UNESCO village, easy walking between estates.
  • Entre-deux-Mers — rolling hills, cheapest tastings.
  • Médoc — prestigious but spread out, rent a bike.

Where to eat

Bordeaux's covered Marché des Capucins for €12 oyster + glass of white. In Saint-Émilion, L'Envers du Décor offers a €24 lunch menu.

Travel times from Paris

2h05 by TGV (book 3 months ahead for €25 fares).
