15 Best Cafés in Paris for Students (Wi-Fi, Quiet, Cheap)

📅 Updated May 2026 👤 GlobalSilentWalks Team ⏱ 8 min read

Paris cafés are not all created equal when you need to write an essay. After a year of testing dozens, here are the 15 spots our student readers swear by — with Wi-Fi tested, plugs counted and coffee priced.

336×280 In-Article Ad — AdSense Ready

Left Bank picks

  • Café Coutume (7th) — specialty coffee, fast Wi-Fi, big tables.
  • Strada Café (5th) — quiet, plug at every seat, near the Sorbonne.
  • Treize au Jardin (6th) — calm, brunch-friendly, espresso €2.50.

Right Bank picks

  • Anticafé Louvre — pay by the hour (€5/h), unlimited coffee and snacks.
  • Hexagone Café (Marais) — cozy, English-speaking baristas.
  • Boot Café — instagrammable, small but reliable Wi-Fi.

Montmartre and the North

  • KB CaféShop — local roasters, plenty of plugs.
  • Lomi (19th) — barista training school, the best espresso in Paris.

Around the universities

Near Sciences Po: Café Verlet. Near Nanterre: Atelier des Lilas. Near Cité Universitaire: Le Centquatre public space (free Wi-Fi, no purchase needed).

What to order

Ask for un café allongé (espresso lengthened with hot water). It's €2.50-3.50 and lets you stay longer than a cappuccino (€4.50+).

Café etiquette in Paris

Order at the counter to pay 30% less. Don't camp 4 hours on a single espresso during lunch rush. Tip with rounded coins, not 18%.
