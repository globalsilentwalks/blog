Nice Travel Guide 2026 — The Capital of the French Riviera

📅 Updated May 2026 👤 GlobalSilentWalks Team ⏱ 10 min read 🏷 France

Nice is the unofficial capital of the French Riviera and one of the most dazzling places in the Mediterranean — a city of pebble beaches, palm-lined promenades, Italianate old-town squares and Belle Époque architecture. With 300 days of sunshine a year, an international airport ten minutes from the center and the Alps within a 90-minute drive, Nice is also one of the easiest French cities to fall in love with on a first visit.

Best places to visit in Nice

  • Promenade des Anglais — The legendary 7-kilometer seafront walk, bike lane and roller-skate paradise.
  • Vieux Nice (Old Town) — Italianate alleys, the Cours Saleya flower market, ochre facades and the city's best socca.
  • Colline du Château — A free park on a hill with a waterfall, Roman ruins and the most photographed view of Nice.
  • Place Masséna — The grand red-and-white square that connects old and new Nice.
  • Musée Matisse & Musée Marc Chagall — Two of the best small museums in France, both free for under-26 EU residents.
  • Mont Boron — A wooded headland with hiking trails and stunning sea views — a local favorite for jogging at sunset.

Top tourist attractions

  • Russian Orthodox Cathedral (largest outside Russia)
  • Place Garibaldi & Port Lympia
  • Parc Phoenix (tropical garden + giant greenhouse)
  • Cimiez archaeological site and Roman amphitheater
  • Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art (MAMAC) with rooftop view
  • Day trips: Monaco (25 min by train), Èze, Cannes, Antibes

Student life in Nice

Université Côte d'Azur clusters around 30,000 students across multiple campuses, and Nice's lifestyle is famously good for international students. Rents are higher than Lyon or Marseille (€550–800 for a studio in central Nice) but are partially offset by CAF housing aid and the unbeatable climate. Student transport pass: €30/month, valid on the entire network including the new tram line.

Transportation tips

Three tram lines (T1, T2, T3) cover the whole city — a single ticket is €1.50, day pass €5. The new T2 line runs from the airport to the port in 26 min for €1.50, replacing all the old shuttle buses. Nice is also bike-friendly with the Vélo Bleu share scheme. From Nice-Côte d'Azur airport, you are in the city center in 15 min by tram.

Budget tips

Nice is more expensive than Lyon or Marseille but cheaper than Paris. Budget €55–75/day as a backpacker, €120–160 mid-range. Beaches are free (bring a mat — pebbles, not sand). The €5 Riviera Pass day-trip combo with TER trains lets you visit Monaco, Antibes and Cannes the same day.

Food & restaurant recommendations

  • Socca — the chickpea pancake invented in Nice. Try Chez Pipo (5€).
  • Salade niçoise — the real one has no potatoes and no green beans; tuna, anchovies, eggs and raw vegetables only.
  • Pissaladière, pan-bagnat, daube niçoise — order at any cantine in Vieux Nice.
  • Cours Saleya morning market for produce, evening market for art and crafts.
  • Italian gelato everywhere — Fenocchio in Vieux Nice has 96 flavors including lavender and tomato.

Hidden gems most travelers miss

  • Villa Arson — a brutalist contemporary art school with free exhibitions and panoramic terraces.
  • Coulée Verte — the green park built on an old riverbed, perfect for an evening stroll.
  • Plage de la Réserve — a tiny tucked-away beach east of the port.
  • Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat coastal walk — one of the most beautiful free hikes on the Riviera (35 min by bus).
  • Le Plongeoir — a former diving board converted into one of the most scenic restaurants on the coast.

Frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Nice?

3 days for the city, 5 to combine with day trips to Monaco, Èze and Antibes.

Are Nice's beaches sandy?

No — pebbles. Bring a thick mat or rent a sunbed. The water is crystal clear.

What's the best time to visit Nice?

May, June, September and October. July and August are spectacular but expensive and packed.

Is Nice a good city for students?

Excellent quality of life and climate, growing tech and biotech sectors, but rent is higher than other French university cities.

How easy is it to visit Monaco from Nice?

Very — train every 20 min, 25 minutes, €4.90 each way. Avoid driving; parking in Monaco is brutal.

Conclusion

Nice combines big-city amenities with small-city walkability and small-town friendliness. Whether you visit for a long weekend or settle in for a semester abroad, the Mediterranean light here changes how you see everything.
